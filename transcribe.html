DREAM Lab Audio Transcription gemini-3.5-transcribe

Browser-based audio transcription with word-level timestamps and speaker diarization via the DREAM Lab AI Gateway.

How your files are processed: Audio files are decoded and split into overlapping chunks entirely in your browser, then sent directly to Google's gemini-3.5-transcribe model through the DREAM Lab AI Gateway. Neither your audio files nor the resulting transcripts are stored by this app or on DREAM Lab servers after each request completes—everything stays on your computer until you download or close the page. (See the AI Gateway data privacy notes; do not use with P3/P4 protected data.)
